# Governance

BorderCut currently uses a maintainer-led governance model.

Maintainers are responsible for reviewing contributions, protecting compatibility, managing releases, and enforcing project policies. Routine decisions are made through review discussion and documented in the resulting change. Changes to the public API, portable algorithm contract, fixture format, licensing, or governance require explicit maintainer approval and corresponding documentation.

Contributors who demonstrate sustained, constructive participation may be invited to become maintainers. Maintainer access follows least-privilege principles and may be removed after prolonged inactivity or policy violations.

If maintainers disagree, they should first seek consensus using test results, compatibility evidence, and the project's documented goals. If consensus cannot be reached, the existing behavior remains until a maintainer records a reversible decision with its rationale.
